A small site should not have to build its own login. Its owner picks what they want, a password or a password and a six-digit code, copies one snippet and pastes it in. Somebody else keeps the users and checks the codes.

In February 2017 I thought that was a new idea. It was already two businesses. [Auth0](https://auth0.com/blog/announcing-auth0/) had launched four years earlier, and its paste-in login box was called Lock. Authy sold the six-digit code as an API, and [Twilio had bought it](https://www.twilio.com/blog/2015/02/ahoy-authy-joins-twilio.html) in February 2015. I had heard of neither.
